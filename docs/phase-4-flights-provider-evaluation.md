## Conclusion

**No new APPROVED source was found.** Aviasales/Travelpayouts remains the only genuinely free,
non-billable, ToS-defensible flight-price source, and its status is unchanged from phases 2–3:
**CONDITIONALLY APPROVED** — safe for interactive search, not yet cleared for unattended background
polling. This phase's implementation work (§3 below, in the completion report) proceeds on that
basis: strengthen what Aviasales and the mock provider can already honestly show, rather than
integrate anything new. `docs/provider-policy.md` is the authoritative, code-synced record;
this document is the research trail for Phase 4 specifically.
